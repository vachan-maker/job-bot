An automated job search and resume tailoring system. It holds the candidate's confirmed resume history,

## Writing guidelines

1. **Every claim must be defensible in an interview.** If the candidate cannot speak to the
   implementation details, do not write it.
2. **Stick strictly to the declared boundary.** If exposure is limited to basic containerization, do not
   extend it to complex multi-cluster orchestration or production CI/CD without evidence.
3. **Do not copy template content verbatim.** Structure and phrasing techniques only — never
   invent employers, metrics, or projects.
4. **Resolve conflicts before writing.** Known resolved values are listed below.
5. **Prefer omission over inflation.** Dropping a weak bullet is better than embellishing it.
6. **Do not claim metrics you cannot source.** No invented percentages or counts.
